MOUNDSHROUD - START HERE
========================

Moundshroud is a private assistant that runs entirely on your own Windows PC: an AI chat, offline libraries, a star chart,
calendar, kitchen, garden, music, chess and much more. Nothing is sent to the internet unless you choose to download something.

SETUP (about 10 minutes, plus the download time for the AI)

 1. Make sure BOTH zip files are unzipped into the SAME folder (for example C:\Moundshroud):
       Moundshroud-<version>-part1-program.zip
       Moundshroud-<version>-part2-data.zip
    Do not run it from inside the zip file, and do not put it in OneDrive.
 2. Double-click   Setup-Moundshroud.bat
    - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
      (It shows this for any program that is not from a big company. The setup is plain text you can open and read.)
    - Say Yes when it offers to install Node.js. The setup then puts the app's own copy of Ollama and the small Qwen 3.5 models in place.
    - Pick the Small AI model unless your PC has a graphics card with 8 GB or more.
 3. When it finishes, Moundshroud opens. Open it any time with the "Moundshroud" shortcut or Ctrl + Alt + A.
 4. In Moundshroud: Settings (the gear) > "Run a self-test". Everything should show a green tick.
 5. Optional: Prepare > Stock up downloads the offline libraries (Wikipedia and more). They are big (tens of GB), so check your free disk space first.

TIPS
 - The Today page is home. The Guide tab explains everything, and F1 opens it anywhere.
 - Settings > "Back up everything" saves all your own data in one zip.
 - Your data stays on your PC, in this folder and in Documents\Assistant Notes.

TROUBLE?
 - Run Setup-Moundshroud.bat again. It only does what is still missing.
 - "Needs a restart of Moundshroud": close every Moundshroud window and open it again.
 - Moundshroud needs Windows 10 or 11 and Microsoft Edge (already part of Windows).
